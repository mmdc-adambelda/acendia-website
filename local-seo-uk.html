What This Service Covers

Local SEO is the work that decides whether your business shows up when someone searches for your service alongside a location — "[service] near me," "[service] in [town]," or a search that surfaces Google's Local Pack (the map and three-listing block above the standard results). It's a genuinely different discipline from national SEO: Google Business Profile, citation consistency, and reviews carry as much weight as the website itself, sometimes more.

Acendia runs Local SEO as a standing service, not a one-off setup: Google Business Profile management, local landing page build-out, citation and NAP consistency, a compliant review-request process, local content, local link-earning, and rank tracking specific to your actual service area — reported monthly against real enquiry data, not just position numbers.

Who Needs Local SEO

If a meaningful share of your customers search for your service alongside a place name, this is usually one of the highest-leverage investments available: single-location businesses (clinics, shops, trades), multi-location operators who need each site treated as genuinely distinct, and service-area businesses (mobile trades, home services) whose customers search by postcode or town rather than a fixed address. Purely national or online-only businesses generally get more value from the broader SEO agency UK service instead — see our how to rank higher on Google UK guide for how to tell which one actually describes your business.
